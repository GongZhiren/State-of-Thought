# State-of-Thought v1.0.0

This is the first public implementation release accompanying
[State of Thought Enables Endogenous Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16055).

## Included

- End-to-end controller fitting and inference for three language-model and two
  vision-language-model backbones.
- Frozen, hash-locked controller checkpoints and training trajectories.
- Publication-aligned result summaries and curated per-example records.
- Training-free, embedding-based, and trajectory-judge extensions.
- Reproducible experiment configurations, validation tools, tests, and
  structured documentation.

Backbone weights and datasets are not redistributed and remain subject to
their original licenses and access terms.
